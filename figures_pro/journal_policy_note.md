# Nature Water Figure-Policy Note

Checked on 2026-06-09 against current Nature Water / Nature Portfolio pages.

## Nature Water Article Constraints

- Article is the original-research format for Nature Water.
- Article length: up to 3,000 words excluding abstract, Methods, references and figure legends.
- Abstract: up to 150 words, unreferenced.
- Display items: up to 7 figures and/or tables.
- Structure: Introduction without heading, main text, Discussion/Conclusions, Methods.
- Supplementary Information is allowed when relevant to understanding or replication.

Source: https://www.nature.com/natwater/content

## Double-Anonymized Review

Nature Water offers double-anonymized peer review. If selected, the manuscript
must conceal author identities and the submission system option must be ticked.
Author names, affiliations and contact details should be moved to the cover
letter rather than the manuscript file.

Sources:

- https://www.nature.com/natwater/submission-guidelines/dapr
- https://www.nature.com/natwater/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-submission

## AI / Figure Integrity Boundary

Springer Nature journals do not permit publication use of generative-AI-created
images or videos except in narrow, labelled exceptions. This figure factory
therefore treats any web-generated image supplied during brainstorming as a
style reference only, not a submission-facing source. Final scientific figures
are script-generated from derived tables, hand-authored SVG geometry and
Blender source scripts.

Source: https://www.nature.com/natelectron/editorial-policies/ai
