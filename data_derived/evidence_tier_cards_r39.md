# Independent evidence-tier cards for regional sign and claim class

## NCP-Beijing

- Claim class: mechanism anchor
- Aquifer context: semi-confined basin-fill recovery
- CSR: positive/recharge-side sign but Beijing is sub-material in global screen
- GSFC: used as independent sign guardrail where available
- GFZ: GFZ raw sign match 2/2; GFZ leakage-adjusted sign match 2/2
- JPL CRI: earthdata_authentication_required_not_ingested
- Local/InSAR evidence: high: >2000 wells and about 190000 measurements in Long et al. 2025; Beijing urban well mechanism in Li/Wang et al. 2025 | high deformation context; regional subsidence/recovery is heterogeneous
- Article use: Shows managed recovery can move the water-table term; not a material global-screen case.

## Tokyo Bay-Yokohama

- Claim class: sign-supported coastal review case
- Aquifer context: coastal mixed/delta-reclaimed review
- CSR: Yokohama CSR-material at S_y=0.10; Tokyo targeted/sub-material depending S_y
- GSFC: Yokohama GSFC sign support but not GSFC-material
- GFZ: GFZ raw sign match 2/2; GFZ leakage-adjusted sign match 2/2
- JPL CRI: earthdata_authentication_required_not_ingested
- Local/InSAR evidence: medium-high sign: Yokohama 20/23 rising; Tokyo official records mostly rising | medium: deformation monitoring context exists; not proof of shallow liquefiable heads
- Article use: Positive coastal case with local sign support; materiality remains product- and S_y-bounded.

## Mumbai-Bhayandar

- Claim class: candidate-only contradiction boundary
- Aquifer context: coastal mixed/delta-reclaimed review
- CSR: CSR-material positive at S_y=0.10
- GSFC: GSFC direction match only; no statistical sign or material support
- GFZ: GFZ raw sign match 2/2; GFZ leakage-adjusted sign match 2/2
- JPL CRI: earthdata_authentication_required_not_ingested
- Local/InSAR evidence: contradictory guardrail: Mumbai station literature supports depletion/increasing depth | medium-high Mumbai coastal subsidence context; not Bhayandar-specific validation
- Article use: Demonstrates why coastal positive screens need leakage and local-well checks.

## Delhi

- Claim class: product-material depletion case
- Aquifer context: alluvial/basin-fill depletion
- CSR: negative/depletion-side material or targeted screen
- GSFC: Delhi is the only GSFC-material support case among six CSR-material units
- GFZ: GFZ raw sign match 1/1; GFZ leakage-adjusted sign match 1/1
- JPL CRI: earthdata_authentication_required_not_ingested
- Local/InSAR evidence: medium-high: CGWB station literature shows long-term depletion across most of DMR except Yamuna floodplains | high heterogeneous groundwater-linked subsidence/recovery context
- Article use: Strongest independent materiality case; depletion lowers one metric while worsening water/subsidence risk.

## Lahore-Punjab

- Claim class: depletion-side regional review case
- Aquifer context: alluvial/basin-fill depletion
- CSR: negative/depletion-side material or targeted screen
- GSFC: sign support varies by city; not a uniform material claim
- GFZ: GFZ raw sign match 2/2; GFZ leakage-adjusted sign match 2/2
- JPL CRI: earthdata_authentication_required_not_ingested
- Local/InSAR evidence: high for Lahore borehole/GRACE evidence; broader Punjab/NW India depletion supported by regional literature | high for Lahore PS-InSAR; medium for broader Punjab SBAS-InSAR
- Article use: Borehole/GRACE/InSAR evidence supports depletion-subsidence coupling; materiality remains product-bounded.
